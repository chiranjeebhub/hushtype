How it works

Three seconds from thought to text.

No window to open, no text to copy. Hushtype lives in your menu bar and types wherever your cursor is.

01

Hold a key

Right Option by default, or Right Command or Fn. Any other key cancels, so your shortcuts keep working.

⌥ option
02

Just talk

Ramble, pause, correct yourself. Your Mac transcribes live while you speak, so it's ready the moment you stop.

03

Let go

Fillers vanish, punctuation appears, and clean text is pasted right where you were typing.

Let's do 6 pm.
Features

Everything you'd want from Wispr Flow. None of the subscription.

Polished by Claude

Removes ums and ahs, fixes punctuation and applies your self-corrections, streamed so it barely adds a beat.

um so yeah let's meet at five, no wait, six pm on uh thursday
Let's meet at 6 pm on Thursday.

Context aware

Casual in Slack, careful in Mail, literal in your editor. Names on screen get spelled right.

Command mode

Select text, hold the key with Control, and say "make this shorter" or "translate to Hindi".

Learns your words

A personal dictionary for names and jargon. Fix a spelling once after dictating and it sticks.

Snippets

Say "my calendar link" and the full text is typed for you.

History

Every dictation, searchable and stored on your Mac. ⌃⌘V pastes the last one again.

Hands-free

Double-tap the key and talk as long as you like. Tap again to insert, Esc to cancel.

iCloud sync

Dictionary, snippets and preferences follow you to every Mac. Updates install themselves.

Built to feel instant

Transcribes while you're still talking, warms up Claude before you let go, and skips it entirely for short clean phrases. If Claude is slow, you get the raw transcript within two seconds.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Hushtype really free?

Yes. The app is MIT licensed and free. Cleanup uses your own Anthropic API key, and Claude Haiku costs a fraction of a cent per dictation. Short phrases skip Claude entirely.

Does it work offline?

Speech recognition does, once the voice model for your language has downloaded. Without a connection Hushtype inserts the raw transcript instead of the polished one.

Which apps does it work in?

Any app with a text field: Slack, Mail, Messages, Notes, your browser, your code editor. It types where your cursor is.

Why does it need Accessibility access?

To listen for the push-to-talk key system-wide, paste the result, and read the text around your cursor for context. Password fields are never read.

How is it different from Wispr Flow?

Hushtype is native Swift, transcribes on your Mac instead of uploading audio, is open source, and has no subscription.

Can I change the push-to-talk key?

Yes. Choose Right Option, Right Command, or Fn/🌐 in Settings.
