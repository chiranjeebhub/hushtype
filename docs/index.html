Features

Talk at the speed you think.

Built in Swift and SwiftUI, so it feels like part of macOS. It lives in the menu bar and stays out of your way until you hold the key.

Polished by Claude

Ums and ahs disappear, punctuation shows up, and “five, no, six” becomes “6”. Short clean phrases skip Claude entirely.

Knows where you are

Casual in Slack, careful in Mail, literal in your editor. It continues your sentence and spells names that are already on screen.

Command mode

Select text, hold ⌃⌥ and say “make this shorter” or “translate to Hindi”. With nothing selected, it writes what you ask for.

Learns your words

A personal dictionary for names and jargon. Fix a spelling right after dictating and Hushtype remembers it next time.

Snippets and history

Say “my calendar link” and the full text is typed. Every dictation is searchable on your Mac, and ⌃⌘V pastes the last one again.

iCloud sync, if you want it

Your dictionary, snippets and preferences follow you to your other Macs. Signed releases keep themselves up to date.

Setup

Ready in about a minute.

A short guided setup does the fiddly parts for you, and each step ticks itself off as soon as it’s done.

  1. Microphone and AccessibilityHushtype notices each grant by itself and restarts when macOS needs it to.
  2. Voice modelDownloads Apple’s on-device model for your language, so dictation works offline.
  3. Connect ClaudePaste your Anthropic API key. It’s checked as you paste and kept in your Keychain.
  4. Try itA practice field for your first dictation before you use it for real.

The real setup window on macOS 26.

Privacy

Your voice never leaves your Mac.

Speech recognition runs on-device with Apple’s SpeechAnalyzer. Only the transcript goes to Claude for polish, with your own key, and you can turn that off.

Questions

Good to know.

Something else? Open an issue on GitHub.

Is Hushtype really free?

Yes. The app is free and MIT licensed. Polish uses your own Anthropic API key, and Claude Haiku costs a fraction of a cent per dictation. Short phrases with nothing to fix skip Claude entirely.

Why does it need Accessibility access?

macOS only lets an app notice a held key system-wide and type into other apps once you allow Accessibility. Hushtype also reads the text around your cursor for context, and never from password fields.

Does it work offline?

Speech recognition does, once the voice model for your language has downloaded. Without a connection, or if Claude is slow to answer, you get the raw transcript within two seconds.

Can I change the dictation key?

Yes. Right Option is the default, and you can switch to Right Command or Fn/🌐 in Settings. Pressing any other key while holding it cancels, so your shortcuts keep working.

How is it different from Wispr Flow?

Hushtype is a native Swift app, transcribes on your Mac instead of uploading your audio, is open source, and has no subscription.
