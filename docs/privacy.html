Privacy Policy

Your words are yours.

Hushtype turns speech into text on your own device. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads. This page explains exactly what stays on your device, what can leave it, and how to turn each part off.

Who we are

Hushtype is an open-source app made by Chiranjeeb Jena (“we”, “us”). The source code for the Mac app is public at github.com/chiranjeebhub/hushtype and the Android app at github.com/chiranjeebhub/hushtype-android, so you can check everything on this page against the code.

We don't run a server that receives your speech, your transcripts or anything you type. The only service we operate is the optional sync server described below, which stores data it can't decrypt.

What stays on your device

Your voice

On Mac, iPhone and iPad, Hushtype uses Apple's on-device speech recognition (SpeechAnalyzer). Audio from the microphone is turned into text in memory and then discarded. It is never written to disk and never sent anywhere.

On Android, Hushtype uses the phone's on-device speech recognizer when Recognize speech on this phone is on, which is the default. If you turn that setting off, or your phone has no on-device recognizer for your language, Android's system speech service is used instead, and that service (usually Google's) may process the audio on its own servers under its own privacy policy.

On-device polish

If you choose on-device polish, the transcript is cleaned up by Apple Intelligence (Apple Foundation Models) on Mac and iPhone, or Gemini Nano on Android phones that have it. Nothing is sent off the device.

Polish with Claude

Polish with Claude is optional and only works with an Anthropic API key that you create and paste in yourself. When it is on, after you finish speaking Hushtype sends a request directly from your device to Anthropic's API (api.anthropic.com). There is no Hushtype server in between. The request contains:

  • the text of what you said (never the audio);
  • the words in your dictionary, so names come out right;
  • the name of the app you're typing into, so the tone fits;
  • when context is on, a short piece of the text around your cursor: up to 600 characters before it and 200 after;
  • for spoken edit commands, the text you selected and the instruction you gave.

Hushtype never reads password or other secure text fields. On Mac you can turn off context in Settings, so only the transcript and your dictionary are sent. Short phrases with nothing to fix skip Claude entirely.

Because the request is made with your own key, it is part of your account with Anthropic and is handled under Anthropic's Privacy Policy and the terms you agreed to when you created the key. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API inputs and outputs are not used to train their models. We never see these requests or Anthropic's responses.

Don't want any text to leave your device? Choose on-device polish, or turn polish off and use the raw transcript.

Sync

Sync carries your dictionary and snippets between your devices. Hushtype Sync is off until you pair a device; iCloud sync is described below. Your dictation history is never synced.

Hushtype Sync (Mac, iPhone and Android)

You pair devices by scanning a QR code or typing a pairing code. There are no accounts. Your devices derive the encryption key from a random secret in that code, and your dictionary and snippets are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they leave the device. Our sync server, which runs on Cloudflare Workers, stores only:

  • a random group ID derived from your secret;
  • a hash of the access token your devices use to write;
  • the encrypted data, a version number and when it last changed.

Nobody, including us, can read the encrypted data without your pairing code. The server doesn't log or keep IP addresses. Cloudflare carries the network traffic and handles it under Cloudflare's Privacy Policy. If you stop syncing on every device, the last encrypted copy stays on the server until you ask us to remove it (see contact).

iCloud (Mac, iPhone and iPad)

Hushtype can also sync through iCloud between Macs, iPhones and iPads on the same Apple ID. On Mac you turn it on in Settings. On iPhone and iPad it is on when you're signed in to iCloud, and you can turn it off in Hushtype's Settings › Sync. Your dictionary, snippets and, on Mac, a few preferences are then stored in your iCloud account through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, and if you choose to sync your API key on Mac, it is stored in iCloud Keychain. The iPhone app never puts your API key in iCloud. This data is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we have no access to it.

Updates

The Mac app checks for updates by downloading a small file from GitHub, where new versions are published. Like any download, GitHub sees your IP address and the app's version, under GitHub's Privacy Statement. Hushtype doesn't send a system profile with these checks. If you installed with Homebrew, updates come through Homebrew and GitHub in the same way. The App Store and Google Play handle updates for apps installed from them.

What we never do

  • We don't collect analytics, usage statistics, crash reports or advertising identifiers.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data, and we don't show ads.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites.
  • We don't upload your audio, keep recordings, or use your dictations to train anything.

This website is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and reads the star count from GitHub, so those services see your IP address when you visit.

Your choices

Because your data lives on your devices, you're in control of it: you can view, copy and delete your history, dictionary and snippets in the app at any time, and deleting the app removes them. You can turn off Claude polish, context, history (Mac) and sync whenever you like. For data held by Anthropic, Apple, Google or Cloudflare, use their tools and policies linked above. You can ask us about or request removal of anything stored on our sync server using the contact below. Wherever you live, including under the GDPR and CCPA, we will help you exercise your rights.

Children

Hushtype isn't directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If what Hushtype collects or sends changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change ships. The history of this page is public in the repository.
